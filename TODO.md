NOI
--
1	Potential Rental Income (PRI)	(Monthly Rent × Number of Units) × 12
2	Effective Gross Income (EGI)	PRI + Other Income (Laundry, Parking) - Vacancy/Losses
3	Net Operating Income (NOI)	EGI - Operating Expenses (Taxes, Insurance, Maint.)

Break even NOI
--
Gross Potential Income	Total possible rent if 100% occupied.
Operating Expenses	All cash outlays required to maintain operations.
Debt Service (Annual)	Your monthly rent-derived mortgage payments ×12.
Cash Flow after Debt	At the break-even point, this value is exactly $0.
